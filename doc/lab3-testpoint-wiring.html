Lab 3 test points: physical pin → breadboard row

Each node has a header drawn as you see it from above (orientation under each heading) and its test-point breadboard. A wire runs from every physical pin to its breadboard row. The number in a coloured pin is the row it goes to. Grey pins are already used by the CAN modules or power, so leave them alone.

On every breadboard: the wire goes into column a, the scope clips onto column c. Rows 1 and 2 are ground: node GND into row 1, jumper row 1 → row 2 → the − rail. LED rows: 470 Ω from e across the centre gap to f, LED long leg (anode) in j, short leg (cathode) in the − rail.

Before power-up

  1. Wire with every supply off. Leave the CAN module wiring (SPI, CS, INT, 3V3) as it is; none of these pins overlap it.
  2. Nothing else may stay on PB4 (CN10-27 / D5): no servo, no L298N ENA. It carries SRV on N2 and PWM_OUT on N3.
  3. Check continuity from breadboard row 1 to the node's GND, and that row 1, row 2 and the − rail are one net.
  4. The handout's shared GND wire between the Pi, both Nucleos and the bus breadboard must already exist.
  5. The bus breadboard needs its own A_H, A_L, B_H, B_L probe rows; they are separate from these three boards.
